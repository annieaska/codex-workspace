L107: Average ceramides levels (with standard deviation) measured in 20 tape strips at baseline were 2.85 pmol/µg protein (0.70 pmol/µg) which rose to 3.80 pmol/µg protein (0.71 pmol/µg) post intervention—representing a 33% increase (p ≤ 0.0001). 358 ceramide species measured were assigned to their respective classes^{cite90†22 } with class sum values calculated at both timepoints. For 9 of the 12 classes, statistically significant increases were seen in the levels post intervention (Table cite91†2 ).
L108: The molar ratio among lipid groups of Cer:FFA:CHE is approximately 1:18:10 in our measures. L126: cite97†Full size image L193: Visual assessment of skin dryness and erythema was conducted by a trained evaluator on a scale from 0 to 4^{cite127†46 }. Thirty seven subjects with visual dryness of 2.0–3.0 and erythema of ≥ 1.0 at the baseline visit were enrolled in the study. Corneometer assessments were taken, after an acclimation period of 15 min in an environmentally controlled room using the Courage and Khazaka Multi-Probe Adaptor Corneometer (MPA 6). Five readings were taken at each test site at both study timepoints.
L194: ### Stratum corneum cohesivity assessment
L195: Stratum corneum cohesivity was assessed as described previously^{cite128†47 } for all study subjects. D-Squame tapes (D100, 22 mm diameter, Clinical and Derm LLC) were applied to the designated area, pressed firmly onto the skin for 2 s with a D-Squame Pressure Instrument (Clinical and Derm D-500) to ensure constant pressure for each tape, then removed using forceps. Sequential tapes were taken from the same site then stored at − 20 °C.
L196: Total proteins were extracted from the tapes^{cite128†47 } and assayed using the Pierce BCA assay kit (Rockford, IL, USA).
L197: ### Sample collection and extraction of stratum corneum lipids
L198: Stratum corneum lipids were analysed from a population subset (n = 18) at both study timepoints. Cholesterol, ceramides, and FFAs were measured from samples collected using Leukoflex tape strips (2.5 cm × 8 cm) (BSN Medical, UK). Up to 20 sequential tapes were collected from each site and stored at − 20 °C prior to extraction. All tapes were collected, stored and extracted individually.
L199: FFA and Cholesterol analyses were performed on pooled sets of 5 tapes, whist ceramide analyses were performed on a pooled sample from all 20 tapes taken at a specific site. Detailed methods for the extraction, and quantification of FFA, cholesterol and ceramides can be found in cite88†Supplementary Information .
L200: ### Microbiome sample collection and processing
L201: 
L202: Buffer washes were collected from all participants using a sterile Teflon sampling ring using the cup scrub method^{cite129†48 } as previously described^{cite72†9 }. Details of DNA extraction, library preparation, sequencing, informatics processing and qPCR are outlined in cite88†Supplementary Information .
L203: ### Microbiome network of the population (MNP) analysis
L204: Co-occurrence network analysis was carried out using the Microbiome Network of the Population (MNP) method on QIIME2 generated ASV tables collapsed at species level. Taxa were filtered based on 0.01% relative abundance and 37% prevalence thresholds. Networks were inferred using Sparse InversE Covariance estimation for Ecological Association and Statistical Inference (SPIEC-EASI version 1.0.7)^{cite130†49 }.
L205: The neighbourhood method was chosen^{cite131†50 } and the StARS (Stability Approach to Regularization Selection) method^{cite132†51 } used with a lambda max threshold of 0.01.
L206: Reproducibility of the network inference was performed using randomly resampled subsets. Sample subsets were resampled at the 80% level and used to generate 1000 networks. Mean values of the network connectivity and robustness features from the networks were compared using bootstrap hypothesis testing^{cite133†52 }.
L207: ### Microbiome network of an individual (MNI) analysis
L208: 
L209: Networks for individual samples within the population were calculated using the microbiome network of an individual (MNI) method^{cite134†53 }. Networks generated via this method with an individual sample removed, are subtracted from the MNPs of the whole sample set, resulting in a network specific for that sample.
L210: ### Network connectivity and robustness calculations
L211: Network connectivity measures were calculated using the R package igraph (version 1.2.4.1)^{cite135†54 }. Network fragility was simulated by removing nodes sequentially and calculating the remaining size of the largest connected component^{cite136†55 ,cite137†56 }. Nodes were removed based on decreasing betweenness centrality and node degree and the stability assessed by natural connectivity; the average eigenvalue of the graph adjacency as the graph reduces in size.
L212: The robustness score was calculated as the Area Under the Curve (AUC) of the network fragility values.
